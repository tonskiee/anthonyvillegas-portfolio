Project image

MCC FIMS

MCC-FIMS is defined according to the phases of the Software Development Life Cycle. During the Planning phase, the system is intended to serve as a centralized digital platform for the Facilities Office of Mandaue City College, replacing manual logbooks with a verified workflow. In the Analysis phase, the study focuses on identifying inefficiencies in existing reporting methods and determining the requirements for a mediated data entry process that ensures accuracy before reports are committed to the SQL database. The Design phase encompasses the creation of an Administrative Command Center, lifecycle tracking features, duplicate detection logic, and analytics dashboards to provide administrators with actionable insights. The Development phase involves coding these components into a functional system, while the Testing phase ensures reliability through stress tests, usability evaluations, and error detection procedures. The Implementation phase covers the deployment of MCC-FIMS within the Facilities Office, including staff training and system adoption. Finally, the Maintenance phase is limited to monitoring usage, generating monthly reports, and refining functionalities based on institutional feedback.
